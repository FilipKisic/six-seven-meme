Privacy Policy – Six Seven

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Six Seven ("the App") is a free entertainment app. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No account registration is required.

A tap counter is stored locally on your device only (via shared_preferences) to determine when to show interstitial ads. This data never leaves your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalised or non-personalised ads, depending on your consent choice.

Data AdMob may collect includes: advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID), IP address, device information, and app usage data.

You are shown a consent prompt on first launch. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time by reinstalling the App.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
